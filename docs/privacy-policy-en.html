Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 25, 2026

At La Sexta App, we are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your personal information. This Privacy Policy describes what data we collect, how we use it, and the rights you have over it.

By using our application, you agree to the practices described in this policy. We recommend that you read it carefully.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Data Provided by the User

When you register with La Sexta App, we collect the following information:

  • Name: To personalize your experience within the application.
  • Email address: For account creation, authentication, and service-related communications.
  • Password: Stored in encrypted form to protect access to your account.

1.2 Data Generated Through App Usage

  • Ticket information: Data about tickets purchased or used.
  • Points history: Record of points earned and redeemed.
  • Notification preferences: Your settings for receiving notifications.

1.3 Device Permissions

Camera: La Sexta App requests access to your device's camera exclusively to scan QR codes related to tickets and events. We do not capture, store, or transmit images or videos from your camera for any other purpose.

Notifications: We request permission to send you push notifications with relevant information about events, tickets, and service updates. You can disable notifications at any time from your device settings.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Create and manage your user account.
  • Process and manage your tickets and reservations.
  • Administer the points and rewards system.
  • Send you notifications about events, promotions, and service updates.
  • Improve the functionality and user experience of the application.
  • Respond to your inquiries and support requests.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. La Sexta App does not market, rent, or exchange your personal data with third parties for commercial or advertising purposes.

Your data may be shared only in the following cases:

  • Service providers: We use hosting services (Render) to host our infrastructure. These providers are contractually obligated to protect your information.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose information if required by law, court order, or to protect our legal rights.

4. Data Security

We implement technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal information:

  • Encryption in transit: All communications between the application and our servers are made via HTTPS (TLS/SSL).
  • Encrypted passwords: Passwords are stored using secure hashing algorithms. We never store passwords in plain text.
  • Restricted access: Only authorized personnel have access to user data, and only for legitimate operational purposes.
  • Secure infrastructure: Our servers are hosted on platforms with recognized security certifications.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal information as long as your account remains active and is necessary to provide our services. Specifically:

  • Account data: Retained while you maintain an active account.
  • Ticket history: Retained for your reference and to comply with legal or accounting requirements.
  • Deleted data: When you request deletion of your account, we proceed to delete or anonymize your personal information within a maximum of 30 days, unless legal obligations require its retention.

6. Your Rights

As a user, you have the following rights over your personal data:

  • Access: You can request information about the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification: You can update or correct your information directly from the profile section of the application.
  • Deletion: You can request deletion of your account and personal data by contacting us directly.
  • Objection: You can object to the processing of your data for specific purposes.
  • Portability: You can request a copy of your data in a structured format.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the information provided at the end of this policy.

7. Children's Privacy

La Sexta App is not directed at children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from children under this age. If we discover that we have collected information from a minor without verifiable parental consent, we will proceed to delete such information.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. We will notify you of significant changes through the application or by email. The "Last updated" date at the beginning of this document indicates when the most recent modifications were made.

9. Contact

Have Questions or Concerns?

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to exercise your rights, you can contact us:
